About Tea Shop

We began as a neighborhood tea stall and have grown into a cozy spot serving handcrafted teas and light bites. Come enjoy seasonal blends and friendly service.

Our Story

What started as a small tea cart on a busy corner has blossomed into the neighborhood's favorite gathering spot. Every cup we serve is brewed with care — from hand-picked loose leaves to perfectly steamed milk.

We believe great tea brings people together. Whether you're grabbing a quick Milk Tea on the go or settling in with a Matcha Latte and a good book, we want you to feel right at home.

Our menu celebrates flavors from across the world — classic Indian chai, Taiwanese boba, Japanese matcha, and fruity iced blends — all made fresh, every single day.
